# TF-012 — Conduct Controlled External Beta and Triage

**Status:** blocked  
**Owner:** product owner, QA lead, content owner, release owner  
**Depends on:** TF-011  
**Blocks:** App Store candidate decision  
**Primary evidence:** `quality/evidence/YYYY-MM-DD-tf-012-external-beta-summary.md`

## Task description

**Summary:** Run a small, privacy-conscious external beta, triage every credible
finding, and issue an evidence-based decision.

**Expected change:** As a practitioner, I can test Anjali in real life and know that
my cultural, accessibility, and defect feedback will be reviewed by accountable
people.

**What and why:** External beta should validate trust and usability, not chase
vanity participant/retention metrics. The app has no analytics, and tester
personal data must stay outside Git.

**How:** Approve a 20–30-person invited cohort, send accurate instructions,
monitor TestFlight/email feedback and crashes, triage P0–P3 in the repository,
pause on defined stop conditions, iterate through TF-005 for binary changes,
and publish an aggregate final summary.

## Subtasks

### TF-012.1 — Design and approve the cohort

**Description:** Summary: choose a bounded set of testers aligned with test
objectives. Expected change: as product/content owners, we receive useful feedback
from varied devices, accessibility needs, and Hindu traditions. What/why:
uncontrolled distribution before learning from a small group increases harm and
support load. How: define objectives, selection criteria, cohort size, owners,
privacy/contact handling, and stop authority.

**Procedure:**

1. Set initial target of 20–30 invited practitioners.
2. Seek varied iPhone/iPad models, supported OS versions, accessibility needs,
   language/script familiarity, and traditions.
3. Define objective: cultural trust, core usability, offline/reminder/device
   behavior.
4. Store names/emails/consent outside Git in approved system.
5. Assign feedback, content, QA, and emergency-pause owners.
6. Obtain product/content/privacy approval.

**Done when:** Approved cohort plan has objectives, ceiling, coverage, owners,
and privacy handling.

**Evidence:** Aggregate selection matrix and approvers; no personal list.

**Stop/escalate:** Do not invite without feedback/stop owners or privacy plan.

### TF-012.2 — Prepare and send invitations

**Description:** Summary: invite only approved testers with exact build context.
Expected change: as a tester, I know the beta nature, how to install, what to test,
and where to report concerns. What/why: raw TestFlight invitations lack project-
specific expectations. How: verify build/group, add approved emails in small
wave, send canonical What to Test and contact/privacy information, and record
aggregate delivery.

**Procedure:**

1. Reconfirm TF-011 build approval/expiration.
2. Add first-wave testers to closed external group; no public link.
3. Provide What to Test, feedback email, privacy URL, text-only disclosure, and
   beta/confidentiality expectations if approved.
4. Invite in a small batch that support owners can handle.
5. Track aggregate invited/accepted/installed counts Apple exposes.
6. Do not commit personal emails.

**Done when:** Approved first wave can install and has complete instructions.

**Evidence:** Wave date, aggregate counts, packet SHA.

**Stop/escalate:** Wrong build/copy or unapproved recipient pauses invitations.

### TF-012.3 — Collect structured cultural and usability feedback

**Description:** Summary: request reports that can be acted on. Expected change: as a
tester, I can flag a sacred-text concern precisely and see it routed correctly.
What/why: vague ratings do not establish cultural safety or reproduce defects.
How: use TestFlight feedback/email/interviews and ask defined questions with
device/build/context details.

**Procedure:**

1. Ask about exact prayer text, transliteration, meaning, source, deity/context,
   and respectful presentation.
2. Ask whether Today → prayer → completion is understandable.
3. Ask whether Listen availability and self-led Chant/Silent expectations are
   clear, plus saves/relaunch, reminders, airplane mode, VoiceOver/large text,
   iPad layout, and crashes/data loss.
4. Request device, OS, build, steps, expected/actual, and safe media.
5. Route sacred-content concerns to named TF-001 content owner.
6. Do not claim an in-app survey/rating feature exists.

**Done when:** Every received report has sufficient classification/context or a
documented follow-up request.

**Evidence:** Aggregated feedback register and redacted defect links.

**Stop/escalate:** Credible sacred-text error triggers immediate pause review.

### TF-012.4 — Monitor TestFlight crashes and feedback queues

**Description:** Summary: review Apple's diagnostic/feedback surfaces during
active testing. Expected change: as a tester, my crash/report is not ignored.
What/why: unresolved queues create hidden risk. How: named owner checks each
business day, records aggregate counts, symbolizes/correlates crashes, and files
repo defects.

**Procedure:**

1. Review TestFlight feedback and crash metrics each business day.
2. Record build-specific aggregate new/open/closed counts.
3. Inspect every crash/feedback item and link duplicates.
4. File canonical issue with severity and reproduction evidence.
5. Acknowledge direct reports within two business days.
6. Do not add product analytics for beta monitoring.

**Done when:** No untriaged item remains at each review checkpoint/end.

**Evidence:** Dated aggregate queue log and issue links.

**Stop/escalate:** Repeated unexplained crash cluster pauses expansion.

### TF-012.5 — Triage findings and enforce stop conditions

**Description:** Summary: classify risk consistently and pause when trust/safety
requires it. Expected change: as a user, serious content, privacy, accessibility, or
stability problems are handled before more distribution. What/why: cohort growth
must not outrun risk response. How: assign P0–P3, owners, reproduction, and
resolution; emergency owners stop testing/invitations on defined conditions.

**Procedure:**

1. Classify:
   - P0: privacy/content harm, crash/data loss, unusable primary path;
   - P1: major accessibility, reminder-state, persistence, navigation defect;
   - P2: material but avoidable;
   - P3: polish/future.
2. Pause invitations/testing for P0, credible sacred-text error, privacy
   mismatch, repeated data loss/crash.
3. Assign owner and target decision for every issue.
4. Human content owner dispositions every cultural report.
5. Product/QA decide whether P1 is release-blocking and record reasoning.

**Done when:** Every finding has severity, owner, disposition, and pause state.

**Evidence:** Redacted triage register and decision log.

**Stop/escalate:** A model cannot downgrade cultural/privacy risk to keep beta
running.

### TF-012.6 — Manage fixes and replacement builds

**Description:** Summary: ensure changes return through the correct gates. Expected change:
as a tester, a replacement build has the same evidence discipline as the
first. What/why: patching source without new identity invalidates prior
verification. How: route metadata-only changes through TF-004 synchronization;
route binary/content changes through TF-001 if needed and TF-005 onward with a
new build.

**Procedure:**

1. Determine whether finding changes metadata, content, or binary.
2. Metadata-only: update canonical repo, approve, sync App Store Connect,
   record change.
3. Sacred content: return to TF-001 for human disposition/sign-off.
4. Binary/content bundle: return TF-005, select new unique build, rerun TF-006
   through relevant distribution gates.
5. Stop unsafe/superseded build and notify affected testers accurately.
6. Never transfer old GO evidence to a changed binary.

**Done when:** Every released fix follows its required gate path and build
identity.

**Evidence:** Issue-to-task/build traceability.

**Stop/escalate:** Unversioned hotfix or metadata drift is prohibited.

### TF-012.7 — Publish external-beta summary and go/no-go

**Description:** Summary: close the cohort with aggregate evidence and an
accountable next-step decision. Expected change: as stakeholders, we understand what
was tested, found, fixed, and remains open without exposing tester identities.
What/why: vanity ratings/retention are neither measured nor release proof. How:
summarize Apple-available participation, feedback/crash/defect counts, content
dispositions, coverage, open risks, and named owner decision.

**Procedure:**

1. Record build, dates, expiration, invited/installed counts if Apple exposes
   them, and completed feedback responses.
2. Summarize device/OS/accessibility/cultural coverage.
3. Count findings by P0–P3 and status; link canonical issues.
4. Summarize every content concern's human disposition.
5. Confirm TestFlight queues are triaged.
6. Confirm no open P0 or release-blocking P1.
7. Product, QA, content, and release owners sign:
   - expand beta;
   - prepare App Store candidate;
   - replace build; or
   - stop.
8. Update canonical backlog/status docs.

**Done when:** Aggregate summary is complete, privacy-safe, and has named
go/no-go with no hidden blocker.

**Evidence:** `quality/evidence/YYYY-MM-DD-tf-012-external-beta-summary.md`.

**Stop/escalate:** Do not invent retention, star ratings, or unobservable
completion metrics.
